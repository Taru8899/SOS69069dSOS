dSOS Dapp

This build matches dSOSSystem + dSOSOffers (2-file split). The contract never writes to the SOS ledger. Every value-moving call carries your current nonce, which this page reads for you, and every action is simulated first: nothing is sent if the simulation fails. Sellers write their own records to the ledger in the Sell records tab.

Sell SOS records to an A / B offer

1) Write records to the SOS ledger (you sign each one, the creator of the offer is the receiver). 2) Confirm them in dSOS with “Sync records”: each confirmed record moves its slices to your own offer, first come first served. 3) Redeem in My offers before expiry. Each record is +1 Push for you and +1 Trust for the creator.

Credits & stake

Become staked

A 1-block gate offer (G) worth more than the gate requirement, left unredeemed and swept, stakes the donor. The ETH goes to the pool and is not refundable. Cooldown before redeeming credits: 14400 blocks.
