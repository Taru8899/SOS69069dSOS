A gate offer (G, 1-block delay) worth more than the gate requirement, left unredeemed until expiry and swept, stakes the donor. The ETH goes to the pool. Cooldown before redeeming credits: 14400 blocks.
Cost is the full stake requirement, paid into the pool and not refundable. The gate requirement moves with the pool, so the amount may change before you confirm.
