Unaudited contract. Gate offers are a one-way donation: once swept, the ETH stays in the pool. Offers expire: after the sweep block anyone can sweep them and the holder gets nothing. Test with small amounts.

Create offer (one record, any number of slices)

A wallet send only moves slices of offers with more than 7200 blocks (about a day) left. Use a delay above 7200 for offers meant to be sent by wallet.

Send dSOS (wallet-style)

Sends exactly N slices from your newest sendable offers: B/T/P offers with more than 7200 blocks (about a day) left. Splits an offer if needed. Writes no ledger record. To send an offer with less time left, use Transfer in My offers.

Stake (gate offer helper)

Creates a G offer with delay 1 for the smallest whole multiple of 0.001 ETH above the gate requirement. After the next block anyone can sweep it. The ETH is donated to the pool and you become staked.
